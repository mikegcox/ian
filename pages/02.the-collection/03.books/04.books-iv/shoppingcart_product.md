### Collectors Item
#### A very limited supply available.

Volume IV, 1000 Islands is a smaller booklet that was intended to raise the bar on quality for this type of publication. Apparently it was successful, winning a Premier Print 'Benny' Award in the graphic arts industry’s largest and most prestigious worldwide printing competition.
