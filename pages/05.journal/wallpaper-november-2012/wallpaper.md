It’s that time of year when it’s time to share wallpaper images again to ease winter withdrawal. For the first time, it wasn’t the cold that chased me off the island, but low water. When the taps began pouring mud, it became clear (pun intended) that it was time to go. Despite having dug a hole to deepen the intake in the harbor in  anticipation of low water, it still dropped too low with every wave  sliding more muck down the hole. This was a battle I was not going to win.</p>
<p>For those who weren’t on the River to see what fall produced, these were the lowest levels anyone I know has ever seen. Shoals appeared where none had been before and one dawn I awoke to not only my first glimpse of one just off the island, but a bald eagle standing sentry on it! I posted that image on <a href="http://www.facebook.com/oneinathousand?ref=ts&amp;fref=ts">One in a Thousand's Facebook page</a> if you're curious.&#160; Regardless, whether it’s the cold or low water that forces me off the island, it always becomes easy to leave when it's hard to stay.</p>
<p>Last season, these wallpapers moved away from the traditional contest of identifying locations to sharing shots of places more easily recognized. This winter I’ll shake it up with a few of each. I’ll start the contest again using this image, which is worth a set of six 8x10 prints for the best caption which explains where it was taken while also sharing a story or any interesting lore you may know about this part of the River.</p>

It’s not a big problem for <a href="http://www.1000islandsphotoart.com/tabid/423/articleType/ArticleView/articleId/54/Water-Wind-and-Sky-II.aspx"> Volume II</a> and <a href="http://www.1000islandsphotoart.com/tabid/423/articleType/ArticleView/articleId/57/The-very-best-of-Ian-Coristines-1000-Islands-V.aspx">Volume V</a> which are shipped from the US, but for the few  remaining copies of <a href="http://www.1000islandsphotoart.com/tabid/423/articleType/ArticleView/articleId/53/The-Thousand-Islands-I.aspx">Volume I</a>, <a href="http://www.1000islandsphotoart.com/tabid/423/articleType/ArticleView/articleId/55/The-Thousand-Islands-III.aspx">Volume III</a> and giclée prints which have to be shipped  from Canada, cross border mail is notoriously slow despite Canada Post charging a fortune.</p>
