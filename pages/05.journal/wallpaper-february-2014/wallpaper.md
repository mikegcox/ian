<p>Okay, that&rsquo;s about enough of winter. While there have been a couple of thaws along the way, this has been one of those old-fashioned cold ones. Cold enough to freeze the River across its width in many places, reportedly even in the US Narrows, which is unusual. The good news is that this should reduce evaporation and lake effect which can only help with water levels.</p>
<p><span>Thanks to Peter Grevelding, Dave Fownes and Steve Jarvis who all correctly identified last month&rsquo;s scene as Jones Creek, taken a couple of days after one of two ice storms this winter has brought us. For those who don&rsquo;t know it, the 1000 Islands National Park has opened an extensive system of trails here that will let you explore this exquisite nature preserve, year round.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><img style="width: 300px; margin-left: 5px; height: 189px;" alt="Wallpaper January 2014" src="http://www.1000islandsphotoart.com/Portals/ian/wallpapers/1000_islands_wallpaper_january_2014_small.jpg"></p>
<p><span>Access has not yet been widely discovered, but near the eastern end of the Parkway, look for a small parking area on the north side, opposite Browns Bay Provincial Park. If it&rsquo;s summer, you can cross the street after your walk for a refreshing swim at Browns Bay, which offers the finest beach along the Canadian side.</span></p>
<p><span>So, after a couple of months of winter scenes, it&rsquo;s time to start dreaming about summer, particularly because spring is less than seven weeks away. In deference to the realities of the season, I&rsquo;ll stick with a white theme, but introduce a little more green.</span></p>
<p><span>There are a few magical spots on the River that really say &ldquo;Thousand Islands&rdquo; to me, visually. Places I have returned to shoot repeatedly in an attempt to capture a few of the countless moods the River gifts us, if we&rsquo;re paying attention. This is one of those places. It has been my desktop picture since I took it.</span></p>
<p><span>If you know where this is, please share the location and/or any comments or stories this scene brings to mind.</span></p>

Enjoy!
