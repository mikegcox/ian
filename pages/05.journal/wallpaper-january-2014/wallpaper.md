<p>For the last two months, I had intended to share a summer image to help bring those of you who are experiencing winter withdrawal back to the River. Both times, my plan was thwarted at the last minute by images captured just before posting. And so it is again.&nbsp;</p>
<p><img style="width: 300px; height: 189px; margin-left: 0px;" alt="Wallpaper December 2013" src="http://www.1000islandsphotoart.com/Portals/ian/wallpapers/1000_islands_wallpaper_december_2013_small.jpg"> </p>
<p>This is my second winter living on the River. I have to say that in a very different way, it&rsquo;s every bit as beautiful as it is in summer, particularly last week. An ice storm followed by a gentle snowfall provided beautiful scenes in every direction. My thought is that you&rsquo;ll enjoy seeing the River in a way that most of you seldom, if ever, get to see. </p>
<p>I spent an entire day trying to decide between several images, all taken within a minute or two, but all quite different. The next day I went out again and the decision became even more complicated with a couple more worthy candidates added to the mix. I wish I could share them all. Perhaps I will, over time, on <em><a href="http://bit.ly/YhMhXr">One in a Thousand</a></em>&rsquo;s <a href="http://on.fb.me/Kcml9O">Facebook</a> and <a href="http://bit.ly/19u5qc9">Pinterest</a> pages. </p>
