No cigar identifying last month's image, but Ken Herring, John Krake and  Libby Jewsbury's guesses of the view from Waterson State Park's cruiser  dock came very close. The partial island on the right is the western tip  of Ash Island and this view looks towards Ivy Lea, on the way across from the  Rift to the Ivy. Thank you for trying.</p>
<p><img width="300" height="188" alt="Wallpaper November 2012" src="http://www.1000islandsphotoart.com/Portals/ian/wallpapers/1000_islands_wallpaper_November_2012_small.jpg"></p>
<p>It was just one year ago when a 1000 Islands image solved an insoluble problem in my life. Susie Smith had canvassed River photographers for images for a <a href="http://www.thousandislandslife.com/BackIssues/Archive/tabid/393/articleType/ArticleView/articleId/807/TI-Lifersquos-Gifts-in-December.aspx">Christmas gift</a> to TI Life’s readers. Amongst these was an amazing scene which simply blew me away. It was a starry night shot of the River with a panorama of stars overhead. Breathtaking!<br>
<br>
I called Susie and asked for the photographer’s number. I almost never do that. The last time was when I first heard Great Lake Swimmers performing on a CBC radio show. Both calls led to unexpected and amazing collaborations.<br>
<br>
It turned out that the photographer was Doug McLellan, an island owner in the Lake Fleet Group and the CEO of an incredibly brilliant “Apple like” creative team, Toronto’s <a href="http://www.mclellangroup.com">McLellan Group</a>, with his wife Caroline, its VP. Doug had been a successful television producer for entities such as CBC, CTV, PBS and National Geographic before founding McLellan Group to tell stories in magical ways for businesses.<br>
<br>
As it happens, I had just finished two years of intensive effort with Donna Walsh Inglehart (the gifted author of <a href="http://www.troubadourinteractive.com/grindstonedetail1.html"><em>Grindstone</em></a>) forging the final draft of <a href="http://www.oneinathousand.ca/"><em>One in a Thousand</em></a>. I had always envisioned that this true story would use media I’d collected along the way to bring it to life in ways not possible before the digital age. <br>
<br>
I had a decade’s worth of relevant images, HD video from the History Channel (who fortuitously had shot a segment for a show about my TI aerial photography) and thanks to Tony Dekker, amazing instrumental versions of some of the Swimmers' greatest hits. I also had a map, nature sounds and some video of my own that I hoped to incorporate where it would add value and help tell the story.<br>
<br>
But there was a major problem. While the platform to present this had been invented (Apple’s iPad), the single company intent on producing leading edge software for this purpose had vanished. They had showcased their amazing technology through <a href="http://pushpoppress.com/ourchoice/">Al Gore’s book, <em>Our Choice</em></a>. It was so exceptional that Mark Zuckerburg immediately bought the company, closed it down and folded their technology into Facebook. I canvassed App developers around the world, but there were no workable solutions. Nine years after I had begun, this project had hit a dead end.<br>
<br>
Then, this serendipitous connection. Doug believes, as I do, that this is the future of publishing. He mentioned that he saw my other books as a 'gift to the community' and explained; “Caroline and I would like to leave our own gift. I think we can help you with this.” He set his entire team to work a year ago. <em>One in a Thousand</em> launched in <a href="https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/one-in-a-thousand/id518088485?ls=1&amp;mt=8">Apple’s iPad App Store</a> on May 1st. <br>
<br>
If you’ll forgive me for saying so, it makes an appropriate and very inexpensive ($8.99) gift for islanders, particularly as it includes as many images as all my other books combined, plus several videos, 18 unpublished instrumental tracks from the Swimmers and quite a bit more. <a href="https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/one-in-a-thousand/id518088485?ls=1&amp;mt=8">You can get it or gift it (use the drop down arrow beside the buy button), here.</a><br>
<br>
So December’s wallpaper was inspired by Doug and stands as a tribute to him, Caroline and their amazing McLellan Group team who accomplished the impossible, turning my unlikely dream into reality.<br>
<br>

I was deeply honored in 2006 when this <a href="http://www.dxo.com/us/photo/in_the_press?newslang=eng-IN">award-winning company</a> asked if I would be one of their founding <a href="http://www.dxo.com/us/photo/imagemasters">Image Masters</a>.
